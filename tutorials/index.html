Learn Pixl Studio in seven short videos

Each one is under two and a half minutes and shows one thing: your first game, painting a player, animation, sound, the built-in lessons, lists, and taking your work home. Watch them in order, or jump to the one you need.

    2:12

    My games, Save progress and Load progress in the More menu, and sharing a game as a link.
